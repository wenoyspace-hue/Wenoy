How we work.

Plain terms, so you know what to expect.

How a project works

You share your business details. We build a preview. You review it and request changes. Once you approve, we help you launch it. A written quote is agreed before any paid work starts.

Revisions

Changes you request on the preview are part of the project. Large new requests outside the agreed scope are quoted separately.

Ownership

Your website content and your domain belong to you. Domains are registered in your name, and Shopify store accounts are owned by you.

Payments and refunds

Prices, payment dates and any refund terms are written in your quote and agreed before work begins. Third-party costs such as domain registration or Shopify plans are paid directly to those providers.

What we will not build

We decline websites that are illegal, deceptive, fake stores, counterfeit or adult content, gambling, weapons, or that impersonate another business.

Privacy policy

This website does not store your information. When you send an enquiry, the details you type open in WhatsApp or your email app, and are sent only when you press send. We use your details only to reply to you and to deliver your project, and we do not sell them. To ask us to delete anything you sent, email wenoyspace@gmail.com. If website analytics is turned on, it will be provided by a third party and described by that provider.
